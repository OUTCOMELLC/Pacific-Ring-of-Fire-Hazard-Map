How to read this: The weather risk badge is a forecast, computed transparently from atmospheric & marine signals — falling/low barometric pressure, wind & gusts, rain probability, and significant wave/swell height. Tides are astronomically predictable; they matter here only where they combine with high surf to drive coastal flooding. Earthquakes, volcanoes & geomagnetic (solar) activity do not forecast weather — no physical mechanism links them. Think Earth · Fire · Magnetism: a separate seismic / tsunami / ashfall / space-weather layer for awareness only. Wind & Water — atmosphere, ocean & moon — drive the actual forecast.
Sources — atmospheric & marine forecast: Open-Meteo (model; substitutes for NDBC buoys, whose raw feeds block browser access) · tide predictions: NOAA CO-OPS (US stations) · seismicity: USGS M4.5+ past 7 days · volcano alert levels: official observatories (linked).
