Data sources

◉ USGS — quakes & volcano alerts 🗾 JMA 気象庁 防災 — Japan quakes / forecast 🌊 Open-Meteo — weather + marine model 🌙 NOAA CO-OPS — US tide predictions 🛟 NDBC / NOWPHAS — buoy network 🧲 NOAA SWPC — geomagnetic Kp ✈ airplanes.live — live ADS-B 🛢 BOEM/BSEE — offshore platforms
What this is — and isn't. Wind & Water signals (pressure, wind, swell, tide, moon) legitimately combine into a forecast. Earth & Fire signals (quakes, volcanoes, geomagnetic) are a separate hazard track — a big undersea quake is a real tsunami precursor, but none of them predict weather. Aircraft, buoys and platforms are context, not inputs. Kept honest on purpose.
